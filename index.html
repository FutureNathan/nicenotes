how private is it?

Your note lives only inside the link, in the part after the #. Browsers never send that part to any server, so it's never uploaded or stored anywhere. Not by us, and not by our host.

No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Fonts are served from here, so nothing else sees you either.

Anyone who has the link can open the note, so send it only to them. Add a secret word and the note is encrypted; without the word, the link is just noise.

"Fades after" hides the note once that time has passed. Because the note is in the link, that's a gentle promise rather than a guarantee, and nobody can stop a screenshot.
